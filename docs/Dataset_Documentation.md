# Milestone 3: Datasets & Knowledge Base Documentation

## 1. Seeded Datasets
- **`SEEDED_HS_CODES`**: 15 authoritative HS classifications spanning electronics (8471.30.10), solar panels (8541.40.11), chemicals (2804.70.00), garments (6109.10.00), automotive parts (8708.29.00), and pharmaceuticals (3004.90.99).
- **`SEEDED_REGULATION_DOCS` & `SEEDED_REGULATION_CHUNKS`**: DGFT Foreign Trade Policy 2023, CBIC Customs Tariff Act 1975, IMO IMDG Code, ICEGATE Electronic Filing Mandates.
- **`SEEDED_WEATHER_ASSESSMENTS`**: Sea state profiles for corridors: Chennai (MAA) to Singapore (SGSIN), Nhava Sheva (INNSA) to Jebel Ali (AEJEA), Kolkata (CCU) to Colombo (LKCMB), Mundra (MUN) to Rotterdam (NLRTM).
- **`SEEDED_RISK_ASSESSMENTS`**: 5-pillar ground truth evaluations for calibration and integration testing.
- **`ML_MODEL_METRICS`**: Regression evaluation statistics ($R^2=0.968$, $\text{MAE}=\$84.20$, $\text{RMSE}=\$118.50$).
