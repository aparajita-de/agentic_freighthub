2. How M1, M2 and M3 Connect
M1 — Core Quote Foundation
M1 creates the shipment, validates information, determines route details and calculates an initial rule-based price.
Customer → Create Shipment → Validation → Route / Distance / ETA → Rule-Based Pricing → Draft Quote
M2 — AI Pricing Intelligence
M2 receives the shipment and route data created by M1. A trained ML model predicts freight price from historical
patterns.
M1 Data → Feature Preparation → ML Model → AI Predicted Price → Compare with Rule Price → Recommended
Price
M3 — Risk Intelligence
M3 uses the same shipment context to analyze weather, customs and route risks and produces a composite risk score.
Shipment + Route → Weather Analysis + Customs Analysis + Route Risk → Risk Engine → Overall Risk Score
Key Connection: M1 creates operational data. M2 adds pricing intelligence. M3 adds risk intelligence. The Quote
Engine combines all outputs.

3. Complete End-to-End Process
1. Customer Login
Customer signs in and starts a quote request.
2. Create Shipment
Enter origin, destination, cargo, weight, volume, mode, container and documents.
3. Validate & Store
System validates data and creates a Shipment ID.
4. Route Intelligence (M1)
Determine route options, distance and estimated transit time.
5. Rule Pricing (M1)
Calculate base, distance, weight, fuel and handling costs.
6. AI Pricing (M2)
ML model predicts an intelligent freight price.
7. Risk Intelligence (M3)
Weather, customs and route risks are analyzed.
8. Composite Risk
Risk Engine calculates LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH or numeric risk.
9. Quote Engine
Combines shipment, route, prices and risk into a draft quote.
10. Human Review
Freight Agent approves, modifies, requests information or rejects.
11. Final Quote
Approved quote is sent to the customer.
12. Customer Decision
Customer accepts or rejects; lifecycle is updated.
Master Flow
CUSTOMER → SHIPMENT → M1 ROUTE + RULE PRICE → M2 AI PRICE → M3 WEATHER/CUSTOMS/ROUTE
RISK → QUOTE ENGINE → AGENT REVIEW → FINAL QUOTE → CUSTOMER ACCEPT/REJECT

4. AI Intelligence Layer
AI Pricing Model
ML regression predicts freight price using historical features such as distance, weight, mode, container, fuel and carrier.
Route Intelligence
Recommends suitable routes using distance, transit time and operational criteria.
Weather Intelligence
Analyzes route-related weather conditions and possible delay risk.
Customs Intelligence
Checks required documents, missing requirements, restrictions and customs risk.
Risk Prediction Engine
Combines multiple signals into one explainable composite risk score.
AI Quote Recommendation
Uses pricing and risk outputs to recommend a commercially sensible quote.

Final Summary
This is one connected intelligent freight quotation workflow—not three separate projects. M1 provides the foundation, M2
adds machine-learning price intelligence, and M3 adds operational risk intelligence. AI Agents perform analysis, human
dashboards provide control, and the Quote Engine connects all results into one final business decision.
